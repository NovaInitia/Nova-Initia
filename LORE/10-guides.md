**Guides**

The Guides were the first class recognized after the fall. Their natural curiosity led them to explore the web and they came across pieces of technology scattered around that appeared to link the web together. Their explorations led them to uncover many artifacts and they suffer grievous losses in their exploration of the artifacts they uncovered but they were drawn to find out more and share what they found.

The first attempt to rebuild the links they found led them to develop Doorways which, powered by batteries, could link two sites on the web together providing a temporary one way trip from the first to the second. The batteries could only hold a small charge which limited the number of times Guides could share the information on the sites. For the early explorations this was enough since it let them jaunt about through Doorway chains.

Over time the chains proved limiting and Guide Melvin developed Signposts which allow Guides to link a large numb of sites together in a more permanent fashion. This allowed tour takers to go back and forth between sites, exploring at their leis ure. The signposts were improved over the years with branches so tour takers could pick where along the guides path they wanted to explore. The Guides goal is to explore as much of the net as possible and share their discoveries with their fellow survivors. Guides are the core of the rebuilding efforts. Players rally around their chosen Guide, to support them, or to exploit them to their own ends. By using the Tours and Trips set out by the Guides the Givers and Guardians can spread their tools where others are more likely to find them.

Guides have two tools to use to help others rediscover the paths through the web - Doorways and Signposts. Doorways are used to provide temporary trips to other sites and locations while Signposts are used to assemble Tours which provide as permanent a way as possible way to collect and gather sites into a useful order and pattern so their information can be shared.

The most basic Guide tool is the Doorway which forms a temporary, one way link between two sites. As the Guides skill increases they can connect Doorways together making them almost like a temporary tour. Chaining doorways also makes them the most prominent Doorways on a site. Doorways laid by higher level Guides will last longer than this placed by less skilled Guides. Doorways have been rumored to have a sort of magnetic power over Barrels so Givers need to be careful when placing them on sites with Doorways.

Signposts are the Guides simplest and most powerful tool. Guides place a signpost on a site and then that site can be used to build Tours for other members of Nova Initia to follow. If a signpost isn't integrated into a Tour shortly after being placed it will lose it's charge and disappear. Once all of the signposts for a tour have been placed they can be integrated into a tour. As a Guide gains skill and ability they can add branches from each signposts to their tours so the followers can pick which direction they want to follow.
