**Sg**

The Sg is the name of the official currency of the domain known as the Interwebs. Commonly know as "slug". Affectionately known as "sluggers".

**Etymology**

Shortly after the Fall, the world plunged into chaos. Equipment was inoperative and populations were fragmented and scattered. The survivors, though, were a hardy populace and in 25 AFT, Sigourn Grotenfelt, a noted member of the Guides' caste, developed a prototype for a high-energy cell. She used her trademark metal casing made of unobtainium inscribed with her initials SG. Historians of the after-the-fall era suggest that the common name slug as utilized in everyday usage is connected to the name of similar components used in pre-Fall years.

**Development of Use**

The energy cells produced by Sigourn Grotenfelt were rudimentary contraptions at first, able to power tools for a minimal amount of uses. A notable example was the two-way doorway which literally was only usable twice (once each way). As time passed, in order to cater to the rising demands for stable and efficient energy sources (voiced mainly by the Guides' caste who explored further and further from their camps), new materials were tested and the power cells became more efficient. In the beginning, "slugs" were produced by hand in small "slug heaps", which were privately owned workshops, clustered away from the habitable portion of the camp due to the instability of the materials used. By 150 AFT, the energy shells were mass produced in installations incorporating safety precautions such as the "firefly roof", a very light roofing material which in conjunction with the extremely thick walls allowed for a measure of security. "Slugs" were available in specific denominations depending on use requirements. A survivor of the oldest denomination in existence is the "pieces of eight" slug pouch which could be incorporated into PMSK090 or the "paint-it-yourself" signpost and last for fifty uses. Modern-day cells are stable, dependable, and hardly ever explode during use or storage.
