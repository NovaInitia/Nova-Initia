# Doorways

*Folio 20, category 3 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

**Doorways**

are devices (*constructs*) able to connect two remote locations of the Interwebs. The connection allows a traveller almost instantaneous passage from one location to the other.

**Etymology**

The name doorway is a colloquial term originating from everyday experience i.e. stepping through a door frame (a doorway) mimicking the single step needed to pass through such a construction - hence the symbol for doorway. The official terms used have varied considerably through the ages. The oldest references to have survived the Fall mention terms such as Stargate (The Chronicles of Queen Sobekneferu) and Portal (Leaf and Tree saga). The official term used today is Autonomous Interpaginal Device.

**Development of Use**

Nothing is more characteristic of the time after the Fall than the isolation and loss of contact between communities. There are many studies exploring the effects of the separation on the psyches of survivors. Apart from these psychological effects the Fall marked the end of exploration and commerce and heralded the era of scarcity of raw materials. The invention of the high capacity energy cell (*cf. SG entry*) allowed for the reinvention of irreplaceable devices such as the doorway. One of the earliest models was the infamous two way doorway (the charge on the energy cell was only sufficient for two walks through the doorway). Hundreds of unsung heroes of the Guides caste surrendered their lives and sometimes their sanity to the cause. The caste motto *"The cake is a lie"* harkens back to those dark days. In the year 100 AF Alarick von Stackenblocken invented the first autonomous doorway. Folk tradition has it that the crucial design elements came to von Stackenblocken while he was cooking bacon for breakfast. The upheaval caused by his excitement produced such extensive damage to the kitchen that it had to be demolished rather than repaired. This account has not been confirmed by historical evidence. Professor Dietrich von Stackenblocken great nephew of Alarick von Stackenblocken has been known to vehemently deny this event ever took place. Present day devices are capable of transporting up to 50 travellers with relative safety.

**Controversy**

The advent of the age of transportation has proved a bane for the Union Alliance. As more and more travellers use doorways certain fringe elements disrupt transportation with dire effects for the unwary. The infamous "Scallion spin Incident" as well as the "Badger, Badger, salamander, humming bird" disaster have sparked controversy and originated a flurry of lawsuits against Union Alliance. While legislation as enforced by Marshals is strict there is currently no way to ensure the safety of the destination of a doorway. As a result of this the Union Alliance requires doorways to be equipped with a EULA (Eat Ur Lobes Alone) according to Interwebs Rule No24. (*cf. Interwebs Rules entry*)
