**Placing a Barrel**

1.   Navigate to the page on which you want to stash the barrel.

2.   Click on the barrel icon on the toolbar (default location is the second icon from the left).

3.   In the barrel overlay, enter the number of each type of tool you want to put into the barrel next to each type of tool. If your class is Giver, the barrels you stash can contain a combination of tools and Sg in any combination up to a total of 1000 Sg. Only Givers can stash Sg. For stashing purposes, each tool counts as 10 Sg, so you can stash up to 100 tools. If your class is not Giver, each barrel you stash can contain only one kind of tool (up to 10 of them) and cannot contain Sg.

4.   You can include a message (up to 512 characters long, including simple HTML formatting) for the player that opens the barrel. You enter the message in the overlay, below the the tools section.

5.   At level 5, Givers can place a label (up to 114 characters) on the outside of the barrel. This label can be read by everyone who sees the barrel. The label can also be formatted using HTML, but may not include links or images.

6.   To stash the barrel, click on the â€œstashâ€ button in the overlay. Click on the â€œdismissâ€ button to close the overlay.

7. If this happens, a â€œbarrel failedâ€ message will pop up. The contents of the barrel are returned to your inventory, but the useless barrel is discarded.

8.   There is a limit to the number of tools that can be placed on a page. If the barrels you try to stash exceed the limit, a message will be displayed.
