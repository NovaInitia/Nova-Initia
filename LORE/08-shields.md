# Shields

*Folio 8, category 2 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

1.   Shields are not placed on web pages. Shields are equipped (donned) by the player and protect against traps. Tripping a trap without a shield equipped causes the player to lose Sg.

2.   Shields are donned/removed by clicking the shield icon on the toolbar (default location is the sixth icon from the left).

3.   When a shield has been donned, the shield icon is visible. If the shield is removed/ destroyed, a different shield icon is displayed.

4.   Shields are destroyed (used up) when the player trips a trap, or when a player attempts to place a trap on a page that contains a spider (the spider immediately attacks the trap, and the player attempting to place the trap gets caught in the backlash). If your class is Guardian, a shield can deflect the damage of three traps before being destroyed; if your class is not Guardian, the shield is used up after tripping one trap.

5.   If you are not a Guardian, you must actively don your shield (click the shield icon on the toolbar). If you are a Guardian, shields are automatically equipped (as soon as one is broken, you automatically don a new one).

6.   As with all tools, there is a small chance that the shield will fail, rather than deflect the trap.

7.   The energies of a shield worn by a Guardian have a chance of disrupting any doorway the Guardian passes through. As the Guardian becomes more powerful, this chance increases. (Before going through a doorway, the Guardian needs to consider whether it is better to risk disrupting the doorway or risk tripping a trap unshielded on the other side.)
