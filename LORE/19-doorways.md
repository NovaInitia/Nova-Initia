# Doorways

*Folio 19, category 2 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

**Placing a Doorway**

1.   Navigate to the page on which you want to place the doorway.

2.   Click on the doorway icon on the toolbar (default location is the fourth icon from the left)

3.   In the doorway overlay, in the URL field, enter the URL of the doorway destination. There is also a Hint field where you can enter a serious, whimsical, or misleading hint up to 250 letters to entice players to step through your doorway. In the comment field below, type a message which the player will see after they have passed through your doorway - have another 250 letters to mock him.

4.   Only so many tools can be placed on a page. If you try to place a doorway on a full page, you will be warned, and the doorway will be returned to your inventory. If you are not a Guide, you can place up to five doorways on the same page. Guides, however, can place as many doorways on the same page as the tool limit permits.

5.   At level 10, Guides can connect doorways into a chain. If a player finds a doorway from the middle of a chain, they have the option of being transported to the beginning of the change, instead of to that doorway's actual destination.

6.   To make a chain, the Guide places the first doorway, steps through to reach the target page, then places the second doorway--this time clicking on the "Add to existing chain" check box. To add more doorways to the chain, the Guide continues to step through the each subsequent doorway, and clicks the "Add to existing chain" check box.

**Entering a Doorway**

1.   When you find a doorway on a web page, you will see an overlay showing a picture of an open hatch door on the ground. This is labeled with the name of the player that placed the doorway and a hint (the message the player wrote to entice you to step through the doorway). You will also see " of ", indicating how many doorways have been placed on that page..

2.   Mousing over the doorway overlay shows you a link to the profile of the player that placed the doorway; it also shows a "Next" option that allows you to cycle through the stack of doorways placed on the page. On the bottom of the overlay are two further options: "Step through" and "Dismiss"..

3.   If you select "Dismiss", the doorway is hidden from you, so you will never see it again..

4.   If you select "Step through", you will be transported to the destination URL where you will see a new overlay saying "You stepped through 's doorway!", as well as the comment written by player that placed the doorway..

5.   You can rate the doorway after you have stepped through by clicking on one of the two marks at the bottom of the overlay. Click on the check mark if you approved of the doorway, or enjoyed it, or thought it amusing or clever. Click on the "X" if you disapproved of the doorway, or were dissatisfied, or thought it was lame or tedious.
