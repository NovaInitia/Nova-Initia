# Spiders

*Folio 9, category 2 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

**Placing a Spider**

1.   Navigate to the page on which you want to place the spider.

2.   Click on the spider icon on the toolbar (default location is the fifth icon from the left).

3.   Spiders protect the web page on which they are placed by immediately attacking any trap that is placed there. The spider attacks the trap, destroying itself and the trap, and the player attempting to set the trap is caught in the backlash, incurring damage.

4.   Placing a spider on a web page that already contains a trap will cause the spider to immediately attack the trap, destroying itself and the trap, and the player deploying the spider will be caught in the backlash, incurring damage.

5.   There is a limit to the number of tools that can be placed on a page. If you try to place too many spiders, a warning will be displayed. Also, crowding too many spiders on a page may cause multiple spiders to attack the same trap (essentially making them less efficient).

6.   Spiders have a chance of failing when they attack a trap. When this happens a "'s spider sprang but missed" message will appear.

7.   When placed by a Guardian of level 10 or higher, a spider has a chance of preventing signposts from being placed on the same page.
