**Shields**

"Shields" is the collective name used for the protective equipment used by the members of the Guardian class to protect themselves against and to disarm explosive devices. (When issued to Guardians, "shields" covers not only the protective element, but also includes pliers, clamps, and other tools used to disarm devices.)

**Etymology**

Shields are constructs of metal used to create a barrier between the user and the blast of an exploded trap. Their name is related to their function and does not have any other meaning.
