# Guardians

*Folio 11, category 6 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

**Guardians**

Guardians protect. They protect sites by leaving spiders to prevent the setting of traps. They protect unwary travelers by hunting down and disarming traps.

Some Guardians are called to thwart trap-inclined Givers. These Guardians may focus on a single Giver, studying his habits and anticipating his trapping, striving to always have spiders in place to meet him, preventing the traps from being set, and eventually causing the Giver to turn to leaving barrels instead.

Some Guardians focus on keeping their territory clear of traps. They relentlessly and persistently patrol, placing spider after spider and monitoring which Givers transgress those boundaries.

The spider-inclined Guardians say an ounce of prevention is worth a pound of cure. They have a special gleam in their eye when they speak of "duels" wherein they lure a trap-inclined Giver into spending his entire inventory trying to break through the ranks of defending spiders.

Other Guardians focus on finding and clearing traps that are set despite the vigilance of their spider-inclined compatriots. They, too, might track a trap-inclined Giver, but rather than prevent, they quietly disarm the traps before they cause the intended harm. Their emphasis is on borderless tracking and reaction, rather than territorial stakes.

When questioned, the shield-inclined Guardians dispassionately explain that they are containing and controlling the trap-inclined Givers, keeping them occupied with placing traps, rather than driving them into a frenzy of frustration.

The majority of the Guardians, however, have struck a balance, and both prevent and disarm traps. Their common motivation is to protect.

When Guardians have proven their skill and diligence to the Guild, they are brought into the inner circle, where the Guild shares with them the true purpose of the Guardians: to prevent another Fall. There is much debate on who was responsible, some blaming the Givers, some the Guides. All agree that no single site should be permitted to be overloaded, again. Guardians of the inner circle undergo official rigorous training to use their shields to manipulate and disable doorways, and less official workshops, conducted in the private quarters, on how to modify spiders to seek out signposts as well as traps.

Unlike other classes, which stagnate at the higher levels, or merely upgrade their tools, the Guardians maintain discipline and continue to train their perceptions with the result that eventually a Guardian can sense how many traps are on a page. This unique skill permits the Guardians to better manage their resources, knowing immediately if they need to message for reinforcements when the scope of clearing a page is beyond their current inventory stock.
