# Traps and Trappers

"Red wire goes to this lead, white to this one, black to ground. That should complete the final circuit. Now to arm the mechanism. Gently, gently..." BOOM!!! POOF!!!!

A cloud of white dust and smoke enveloped Tristan as peels of laughter filled the classroom. Eyes squeezed shut, he coughed and gagged and flapped his arms about to try and clear the annoying cloud from around his head. Violette, the instructor for this beginner class of Givers, shook her head in dismay. "How many times must I show you this before you remember? You should be far past this stage by now, Tris." Turning to the class, "That will be all for today. Don't forget to do your lesson tonight. Mentors will be making their selections soon enough and you want to be ready. Now scoot!"

As the small group of young Giver prospects made their way out the door, Violette stepped up beside Tristan. "I am beginning to doubt my choice of apprentice, young man. I saw great potential in you. Still do. I don't understand why you are having such a hard time with things you should already know stone cold. Can you explain this to me?"

Tristan teetered back and forth from foot to foot, mulling over in his mind whether to tell her about Amber. He should be able to tell her anything, but this could change everything. She might even drop him as her prime apprentice. That would be horrible. He had always aspired to become a Giver, but he especially liked traps. He knew that he could protect people by warning them of harmful sites, unstable doorways and the like. Violette was the best. If he couldn't trust her with this... "I've been seeing a girl," he stammered, glancing at her out of the corner of his eye. "She's a Guardian apprentice."

Violette took a step back, a slightly shocked look crossing her rough features. "A Guardian apprentice, you say? Has she a focus?" Some Guardians chose to focus their training on the most effective use of shield technology, while others spent most of their training perfecting their skills with spiders, the bane of all Trappers. "Please tell me she isn't aspiring to become an Interceptor."

"I'm afraid she is. And she wants me to stop training with you or any other Master Giver whose focus is traps. She says 'your kind' are mean and evil and only want to hurt innocent travelers of the Web. I have told her she is wrong about you, wrong about Trappers, but she won't listen. If I don't leave your mentor-ship, she won't see me any more." He was visibly shaken by telling his mentor this news, but also somewhat relieved not having this secret eating away at him.

Violette stood in silence, letting herself settle into a mental calm. After what seemed an eternity to Tristan, she simply said, "Then I guess you have a decision to make." With that she turned and left the classroom.
