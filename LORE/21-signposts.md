**Placing a Signpost**

1.   Navigate to the page on which you want to place the signpost

2.   Click on the signpost icon on the toolbar (default location is the third icon from the left)

3.   In the signpost overlay, enter the title of the signpost (later, when stringing the signposts to make a tour, the title will help differentiate between signposts, so choose thoughtfully). The title can be up to 32 characters long. In the "comments" section, enter the text that will be read by the person taking your tour. The text can be up to 256 characters long and may contain simple HTML formatting such as bold text and links. 4.   Signposts have a chance of failing when you try to place them. If you are not successful, you will see an overlay showing a signpost with the top part (the sign) fallen to the ground.

5.   Please note that while other tools stand alone, signposts are meaningless unless placed and connected to create tours. Unless linked in tours, signposts are invisible and pretty much useless. You gain no XP for placing a signpost.
