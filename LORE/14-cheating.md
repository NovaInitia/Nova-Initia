# Cheating

*Folio 14, category 8 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

Sadly, some people want to take shortcuts to gain power, tools, SG, levels, or influence.  "Shortcut" is how they justify cheating.  We do not tolerate cheating.  If you manipulate the game, whether through scripting, interacting with your own alternate characters, or benefiting from bugs, you are cheating.

Marshals are the enforcement and monitoring force of the game.  In addition to their happier beneficial abilities, they are also authorized to apply temporary or permanent bans, freeze accounts, and remove ill-gotten assets.  The player may appeal a Marshal's actions, and two additional Marshals will review the decision.  If you are temporarily banned for actions, this applies to **YOU**, the player.  If it is found you created a new account, or are playing an alternate account, it will result in further actions against you.  (Suck it up and take the time out--reflect on the actions with which you earned it.)

We discourage the same player having multiple characters (known as "alts"), but permit you to have one main and two alts.  They must not interact with one another.  This means you must make sure you do not interact with your own tools.  Do not set traps on pages where your alt has placed spiders.  Do not take tours or trips that were created by your other characters.  Do not loot barrels placed by your other characters.  Etc.

It is forbidden to create an account for purposes of looting the assets.  If a new account does little more than put out its initial assets then cease play, the Marshals may speak with the player who picked up the assets.

Because the activity stipend is paid based on your having used/interacted tools, if it is found you are using a script to perform the action triggering the stipend, you will be marked for cheating.
