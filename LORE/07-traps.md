# Traps

*Folio 7, category 2 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

**Placing a Trap**

1.   Go to the page on which you want to place the trap.

2.   Click on the trap icon on the toolbar (default location is the first icon from the left).

3.   When a trap is successfully placed on the page, an overlay showing a bundle of dynamite will be appear. Note that occasionally traps can fail when you deploy them. If this happens to you, an overlay will appear of a broken stick of dynamite and the words "Trap failed".

4.   A page can only hold so many of each tool. If you try to place more traps than the page can hold, a message will warn you of that, and the trap will return to your inventory.

5.   If the trap is placed on a page that already contains a spider, the spider immediately attacks the trap, triggering it, and causing damage to the player who placed the trap (deducting Sg). If the player has a shield equipped, the damage is deflected and the shield is used up.

6.   At level 5, Givers can leave a message (up to 114 characters) which will be displayed when the trap is tripped.

7.   When encountered, traps will display the name and avatar of the player that placed them. At Level 10, Givers can leave anonymous traps (their name and avatar image will not show up, so the player who encounters the trap won't know who to thank).

**Tripping a Trap**

1.   When you encounter a trap, an overlay with the message "'s trap was sprung" will appear. If you have a shield donned when you encounter the trap, the overlay shows a broken shield; if you don't have a shield equipped, the overlay has charred edges and shows an explosion.

2.   Tripping a trap without a shield equipped incurs damage (you lose Sg). Tripping a trap with a shield equipped destroys the shield (but you do not lose Sg).
