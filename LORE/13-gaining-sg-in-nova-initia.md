# Gaining SG in Nova Initia

*Folio 13, category 1 — recovered from the v1 `Folios` table.*

---

SG can be gained a number of ways in the world Nova Initia.

The first and most obvious is through using tools. The Union and Guilds encourage people to use their tools by providing SG when tools are used. Every hour that a tool is used will be an hour that a stipend will be awarded for. The Union checks every hour and if a tool was used the previous hour SG will be deposited into the  account of the person who deployed a tool. As class levels are gained the hourly stipend increases. If a persons karma leans towards one tool more than another they will get that type of tool as part of their stipend but they will always get at least 25% of the stipend as SG.

Guides gain SG for each person that successfully completes a tour they created and the tour taker also receives an SG reward.

SG can also be found in barrels left by Givers. No other class can leave SG in barrels so it is a good idea to keep Givers safe and happy so they keep leaving SG for others to find.
